You can return eligible products within 14 days of delivery, and we replace or refund damaged, defective or incorrect products at no additional shipping cost to you. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under Egyptian consumer protection law.

Returns within 14 days

  • Unopened, unused products in their original sealed packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery, where eligible.
  • For hygiene and safety, opened or used skincare is subject to the exceptions allowed by law.
  • To start a return, email hello@betweentwosuns.co with your order number and the items you would like to return. We will send you the return instructions.
  • If you are returning an item because you changed your mind, the return shipping is paid by you, where the law permits.

Damaged, defective or incorrect products

  • If a product arrives damaged, is defective, or is not what you ordered, email us with your order number and photos.
  • We will arrange a replacement or a refund at no additional shipping cost to you. Your statutory rights apply, including the 30-day period for defective products.

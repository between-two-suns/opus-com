# Reviews and creator posts: what each needs (DECISIONS 248)

Two separate things, each with one job:

- **Customer reviews**: the customer's words, through Judge.me, on every product page. Text first.
- **Creator posts**: photos and videos from our creators, in the "In their routine." row (demo: /?view=trust on the
  staging theme), added by us in admin.

Nothing is ever written for the shop. No invented reviews, ratings or numbers.

## Customer reviews (Judge.me)

The card shows, in this order: title, text, then stars · name · date, plus Judge.me's Verified buyer / gifted badges.
The card takes the product's colour.

| Part | What | Judge.me setting |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 3–8 words; shown large | Keep "review title" on |
| Text | 2–4 sentences; the card shows 5 lines, then "Read more" | — |
| Stars | 1–5 | — |
| Name | First name + last initial ("Mariam K.") | Name format: first name + initial |
| Verified buyer | Automatic when the reviewer bought the product | — |
| Gifted | On tester/creator reviews | Turn on the incentivised-review (transparency) badge |

- Photos and videos in customer reviews still work (Judge.me shows them) but aren't designed around: most customers send text.
- **Before launch:** delete the 16 test reviews (Judge.me → Reviews). Also translate Judge.me's own button labels
  ("Write a review", "Filters", "Sort") into Arabic in Judge.me → Settings → Translations.
- Review requests: Judge.me's email after delivery (about 10 days after fulfilment), one reminder.

## Creator posts (admin → Content → Metaobjects → Creator post)

| Field | What |
|---|---|
| Media | One photo or one video (upload in the field) |
| Creator name | As they want to be credited |
| Handle | @handle |
| Product | The product in the post; the tile takes its colour and gets its "Add to bag" |
| Post link | Optional: the original post |

What to ask creators for (also in the creator kit, "Sending us your post"):

- **Vertical 9:16**, photo or video.
- **Video:** 10–30 seconds, product in shot from the first second, MP4/MOV, at least 720 × 1280 (1080 × 1920 best).
  Plays muted on the page; with sound when opened.
- **Photo:** at least 1080 × 1920. Square or 4:5 feed photos get cropped.
- Subtitles on screen if they talk (EN or AR).
- The **original file**, not a screen recording or an app download.
- **Written permission** to show it on the site, every time.

When the first real post is added, the stand-in tiles disappear by themselves. Then delete the two stand-in clips
(Files: bts-standin-serum.mp4, bts-standin-barrier.mp4).

# Reviews: what each review needs (DECISIONS 247)

The design is final (demo: /?view=trust on the staging theme). Reviews come only from real customers through
Judge.me; nothing is ever written for the shop. This is what to set up so real reviews fill the design well.

## One review card

| Part | What | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Stars | 1–5 | Yes |
| Title | 3–8 words | Yes (turn on "review title" in Judge.me) |
| Text | 2–4 sentences, 150–400 characters; the card shows 5 lines, then "Read more" | Yes |
| Name | First name + last initial ("Mariam K.") | Yes (Judge.me "name format: first name + initial") |
| City | e.g. Cairo, Alexandria | Optional custom question |
| Skin type | Oily · Combination · Normal · Dry · Sensitive | Optional custom question (single choice) |
| Verified buyer | Shown automatically when the reviewer bought the product | Automatic |
| Gifted | "Received as a gift" on tester/creator reviews | Required by law and by us: turn on Judge.me's incentivised-review disclosure |
| Product | The reviewed product; the card takes that product's colour | Automatic |

## Photos and videos

- **Photo:** portrait, at least 1080 × 1350 px (4:5). The card shows it square (centre crop); a tap opens it whole.
  Best: the product in hand or on a shelf in daylight, or skin texture after use. No filters, no beauty retouching.
- **Video:** vertical 9:16, 10–30 seconds, at least 720 × 1280 px, MP4/MOV. First frame should show the product.
  Sound optional (cards play muted). The card shows a square crop with a play button and the length.
- **Text only** is a first-class card: a quote card with a large quote mark in the product's colour.

## When the home section appears

- Shows once there are **at least 6 published reviews with 4+ stars** and at least 2 with a photo or video.
  Until then it stays hidden (no empty state on the home page; product pages show "Be the first to write a review").
- Order: newest first among featured; the owner picks featured reviews in Judge.me (aim for a mix: 2 photo, 1 video,
  3 text, across all four products).
- The summary shows the real average and count from Judge.me. Never rounded up, never hand-entered.

## Judge.me settings to turn on (owner)

1. Review request email 14 days after delivery (EN + AR), with a photo/video prompt.
2. Review title on; name format first name + initial.
3. Custom questions: City (text, optional), Skin type (single choice, optional).
4. Incentivised-review disclosure on.
5. Media upload on (photos and videos); moderation on (publish after approval).
6. Arabic language on.

## Arabic

Same rules. Arabic reviews show in Arabic on the Arabic site; English reviews show on both until enough Arabic ones exist.

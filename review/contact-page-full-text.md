## Two things to check (not copy changes)

1. **"Within one working day"** is a promise. You approved it (DECISIONS 232), so make sure whoever answers the inbox can keep it, including after weekends and holidays.
2. **The email address** hello@betweentwosuns.co must be a working inbox before launch. Messages from the form go to the store's contact email in Shopify (Settings → Store details → Store contact email).
